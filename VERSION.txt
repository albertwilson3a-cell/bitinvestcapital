BitInvest Capital — version 8 (12 November 2026)

New in version 8: the client portal is built around the investment
- Every coin now has a BitInvest wallet (deposits land here, withdrawals leave from here)
  and an Investment (earns the admin's weekly rate; returns compound into it)
- Invest: choose any amount from the wallet, minimum $50 (admin can change it under
  Rates & parameters). "Move to wallet" takes part or all of it back at any time
- Portal: live investment value (ticks up every second at the admin rate), total returns,
  earnings per day, countdown to the next payout, wallet vs invested bar
- Investment growth chart (1D–1Y): value at market prices vs "your money in"
- Your coins: a card per coin with invested amount, growth sparkline, wallet, returns and
  Invest / To wallet / Deposit / Withdraw buttons
- Where your investment is heading: projection at current weekly rates, with a
  "what if I invest more" slider and 1 week / 1 month / 3 months / 1 year figures
- Returns paid chart (last 30 days)
- New money joins the investment at the next payout period (shown on the coin card)
- Withdrawals use the wallet only; the withdraw window explains how to free invested funds
- Website pages updated: "invest from $50" replaces the old allocation wording
Also includes everything from version 7.

Note: balances already in accounts stay in the wallet. Clients press Invest to start earning.
