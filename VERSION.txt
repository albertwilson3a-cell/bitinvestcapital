BitInvest Capital — version 5 (2 November 2026)

New in version 5:
- Logged-in visitors see "Go to your dashboard" / "Deposit from your dashboard" instead of "Open account"
- Every login is confirmed with a 6-digit code sent by email
- "Forgot password?" on the login page with an emailed reset link
- New passwords must be typed twice; paste, copy and drag are disabled
- Live market prices everywhere (no "Simulated" labels)
- Withdrawals: email-code confirmation, manual admin review, approve, mark sent with tx hash, or reject
- Admin: Withdrawals queue with risk checks, withdrawal fees/minimums, suspend/restore accounts,
  "Rates & parameters" for interest rates
Also includes everything from version 4 (81+ pages, live chat, markets, earn, learn).
